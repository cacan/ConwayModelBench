Game of Life Rules

Conway's Game of Life is a cellular automaton with simple rules that produce complex, lifelike behavior.

  • ✓ Any live cell with fewer than 2 live neighbors dies (underpopulation)
  • ✓ Live cells with 2 or 3 neighbors survive
  • ✓ Live cells with more than 3 neighbors die (overpopulation)
  • ✓ Dead cells with exactly 3 live neighbors become alive (reproduction)
