Conway's Game of Life

A cellular automaton simulation

Generation: 0
Alive cells: 0

How to Play:

  • Start: Click Start to begin the simulation
  • Stop: Click Stop to pause the simulation
  • Reset: Click Reset to clear the grid
  • Randomize: Click Randomize to generate random patterns
  • Step: Click Step to advance one generation at a time
  • Click: Click on cells to toggle them between alive and dead

Rules of the Game of Life:

  1. Any live cell with fewer than two live neighbors dies (underpopulation)
  2. Any live cell with two or three live neighbors lives on
  3. Any live cell with more than three live neighbors dies (overpopulation)
  4. Any dead cell with exactly three live neighbors becomes alive (reproduction)
