Rules of Life
  • Survival: A living cell with 2 or 3 neighbors stays alive
  • Birth: A dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive
  • Death: A living cell with fewer than 2 or more than 3 neighbors dies

About

Conway's Game of Life is a cellular automaton devised by John Conway in 1970. It's a famous example of emergent behavior from simple rules.
